The desk process

The point is not a national CAD dump. The point is a repeatable packet that can travel from a VFG project card to a council email to an engine question without mixing rooms.

1. Seed list

Use only parks VFG has already published, plus Windemere as the live bid object. Source: skateparkconstruction.com.au/projects. The page itself says the full list and referees are by email to Kieran.

2. Packet before file

Every artefact gets a sidecar before it is saved into a working folder. Required fields: source, holder versus copyright owner, form of access, licence words, train flag, date. If train is not yes, the file does not go in a training corpus.

3. Public harvest

Search the named council for the park, then Your Say / engagement, then minutes, then open data. Write the URL into the packet even when the find is only a location pin. Published concept reports still carry designer copyright. They are context, not a licence.

4. Informal ask, then formal access

Email parks or assets. Ask for as-constructed / WAE drawings of the named existing park, for research and community mapping, and ask what fee or licence they can offer. If that fails:

  • NSW — GIPA Act 2009
  • Victoria — FOI Act 1982
  • Queensland (Windemere) — Right to Information Act 2009

Copies of engineering drawings are often refused on copyright grounds. Inspection only is still a useful fact: the drawing exists, the owner can be named, the packet stays honest.

5. Engine room

The Australian Legal Engine indexes acts you already hold and answers by quotation. Suggested first questions, run locally:

python -m engine ask "can a council give me a copy of engineering plans" python -m engine ask "copyright infringement copy of drawings under GIPA" python -m engine ask "right to information inspection only copyright" --prompt

Do not paste invented section numbers into the packet. Paste the engine citation packet, or a note that you have not indexed that act yet.

6. Training gate

Pass: facts, years, councils, contractor names already published, your own LiDAR of a public park, notes you typed from an inspection.

Stop: VFG construction sets, council engineering sheets, photos of drawings taken during inspection, commercial AI reuse of this lab itself (the Strange But True licence already forbids that).

What stays out of this site

Filled tender returnables, prices, private emails, unpublished VFG CAD, and anyone's personal information. Same public/private caution as the Windemere workspace.
