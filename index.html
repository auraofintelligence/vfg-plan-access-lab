Workbench

Do this from a desk

Read the public list

Eleven named parks on the VFG projects page. Email Kieran only for the unpublished remainder and a research-licence talk.

Open a packet

Council, access statute, source, form of access, copyright owner, train flag. Default train is no.

Harvest published traces

Your Say pages, award minutes, parks listings, GIS. Stop when you have a URL or a nothing-published note.

Ask once, then RTI if needed

Informal council email for as-constructed sheets. Expect inspection-only copies where copyright sits with the designer.

Cite with the engine

Index acts you hold. Ask in plain words. Keep the packet and the quote in different rooms.

Gate training

Only rows with train: yes. Own scans and facts can pass. Construction sets cannot, until licensed.
