MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING

Between Luke Nathan Hayes trading as Strange But True
and VFG Skateparks (Kieran)

1. What this is
We already work together on Windemere Road Park, Redland City.
This note records a licensed local model trained on a small set of parks VFG has already built, so future tenders and maintenance are not from a blank page.

2. Two rooms
Windemere tender files stay in the bid room. This MOU does not change that tender.

3. First parks
Black Head, Cowes, Bathurst, Shellharbour 2018 rebuild.
More can be added in writing, including by text or email.

4. What VFG will try to provide
Native CAD (DWG / DXF / 3D), WAE / as-constructed PDF, who designed, who built, year, and whether copyright sits with VFG or the council.
If a file cannot leave the office, a supervised copy or extract is enough to start.

5. Licence
VFG keeps ownership of its drawings and CAD.
VFG licences Luke to keep copies in a closed folder, train a local Strange But True model, and use that model for tendering, set-out, maintenance and digital-twin work.
Luke will not put the CAD on the public web, hand raw files to a third-party training company, or on-sell VFG designs.

6. What Luke will do
Packet each file. Train stays off until this MOU is signed or Kez confirms the same words in writing. Credit VFG unless asked not to.

7. Money
No fee in this note. A park-by-park fee or share of later work can be an add-on.

8. Time
Starts when both have signed or clearly agreed in writing.
14 days’ notice to end. Raw CAD returned or deleted if VFG asks. Copies already used to train may stay in the local model.

9. No extras
Not a partnership, employment or exclusive deal.

Signed
Luke Nathan Hayes / Strange But True    Date ______
Kieran / VFG Skateparks                 Date ______
