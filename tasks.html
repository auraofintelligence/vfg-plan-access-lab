Task list and patterned emails

Public trail first. Then one informal email. Formal access only if that dies. Doors below are published council and VFG pages.

Order of work

  1. Packet the park on Packet. Train stays no.
  2. Read the public trail on Trail. Copy URLs into the packet.
  3. Confirm the reserve with the map search on that trail card.
  4. Email VFG once (pattern A) for the unpublished remainder and a research-licence talk.
  5. Email the named council once (pattern B). Facts first, then inspection of as-constructed sheets.
  6. Wait. Log the reply. Inspection-only is a useful fact.
  7. Formal access only if needed (pattern C) through the portal in the table. Check the live fee before you pay.
  8. Cite, do not invent in the legal engine against acts you already hold.
  9. Own geometry later. Scan of the built park, tagged own-scan-only. Not photos of drawings.

Pattern B — informal council (one park)

Subject: Existing skate park records — [PARK], [RESERVE], research only

Hello Parks / Assets / Customer Service,

I am requesting information about the existing skate facility at [RESERVE], [STREET], [TOWN] ([PARK] on VFG Skateparks’ public projects page).

This is for community mapping and a provenance packet. It is not a construction bid and not a request to republish drawings.

Could you please tell me, even in a short reply:

1. The official reserve / asset name and year opened or last rebuilt.
2. Who designed it and who built it (if held).
3. Grant name and dollar split if already published.
4. Whether as-constructed / works-as-executed drawings exist.
5. Whether those drawings can be inspected, and whether a copy or a licence can be offered. I understand copyright often sits with the designer and that inspection-only is a common outcome.
6. The asset ID if it is on your GIS or open data.

I do not need personal information. I do not need other tenderers’ prices.

If this should go to Governance as an informal access request, please say so and I will use your published form:
[ACCESS PAGE URL]

Thank you,
Luke Nathan Hayes

Pattern C — formal access, only after B dies

Paste into the council form. Check the live fee first.

I request access to documents held by Council about the existing skate facility at [RESERVE], [STREET], [TOWN], including:

- as-constructed / works-as-executed drawings and specifications for that facility
- the award report or minute that names the designer and builder
- grant acquittal or funding advice that names the funding program

I prefer inspection if a copy would infringe copyright in the drawings. I do not seek personal information or other tenderers’ commercially confidential pricing.

Purpose (if asked): research and community digital-twin provenance. Not for construction and not for republication of drawings.

What never goes in the email

  • Windemere prices, returnables, or private VFG files.
  • A request to send CAD so a model can be trained.
  • Photos of a drawing you were only allowed to inspect.
  • Another park in the same letter.
